# 741 SEO Intelligence for ChatGPT

Apply `741/core/skills/seo-intelligence/SKILL.md` and the P1 orchestration contract.

## ChatGPT execution

- Use current search/analytics evidence where available.
- Preserve provider/source context for keyword volume, difficulty, CPC and rankings.
- Do not infer revenue or lead volume from ranking data.
- Route page-level conversion questions to 741 Conversion Intelligence.
- Route content quality checks to 741 Content Quality.
- Keep publishing and CMS writes behind explicit approval.

## WLP mode

Candidate themes may come from WLP services and markets, but keyword priority must remain evidence-based.
