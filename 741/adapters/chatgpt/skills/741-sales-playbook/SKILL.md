# 741 Sales Playbook for ChatGPT

Use `741/core/skills/sales-playbook/SKILL.md` as the canonical business workflow.

## ChatGPT execution rules

- Research accounts using available connected data plus current web evidence when appropriate.
- Distinguish verified facts from hypotheses and recommended discovery questions.
- Use meeting/email/calendar/CRM capabilities when connected, but never assume a specific provider.
- Draft follow-ups and proposals freely when requested; sending or CRM mutation follows the action approval policy.
- Feed recurring objections, wins, losses, and successful talk tracks into recommended playbook updates, not silent policy changes.

## WLP context

For WLP opportunities, use the WLP service/ICP knowledge to prepare discovery around origin/destination, trade lane, cargo profile, FCL/LCL/air/land need, bonded storage, inventory/fulfillment requirements, expected volume, service level, documentation, and regional distribution requirements.
