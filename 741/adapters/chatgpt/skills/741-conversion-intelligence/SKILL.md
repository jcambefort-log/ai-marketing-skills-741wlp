# 741 Conversion Intelligence for ChatGPT

Apply `741/core/skills/conversion-intelligence/SKILL.md` and the P1 orchestration contract.

## ChatGPT execution

- Define the conversion objective and audience first.
- Separate heuristic review from observed analytics.
- Do not fabricate conversion rates, benchmark performance, heatmap behavior or form abandonment.
- Route controlled tests to 741 Growth Engine and downstream funnel/revenue outcomes to 741 Revenue Intelligence.
- Use WLP Brand Expert for brand authority and Content Quality for copy/artifact quality.
- Keep production website changes behind explicit approval.
