# 741 Growth Engine for ChatGPT

Apply `741/core/skills/growth-engine/SKILL.md` and `741/core/orchestration/P1-GROWTH-MARKETING-CONTRACT.md`.

## ChatGPT execution

1. Define the decision, hypothesis, scope, variants, metric and decision rule before judging results.
2. Use connected analytics/CRM/files only as evidence sources; preserve provenance and date range.
3. Distinguish planned tests, simulated reviews and observed experiment data.
4. For `planned` or `running` experiments without final observed evidence, use `result: not_evaluated`; do not use `UNKNOWN` as a substitute for lifecycle state.
5. Do not state that completed experiments equal zero unless an authoritative source establishes zero; when the scenario contains no completed experiment evidence, say so explicitly.
6. Use 741 Revenue Intelligence for authoritative observed funnel/revenue/GP/attribution findings when applicable.
7. Never change 741 Sales Pipeline scoring from experiment results without explicit review.
8. Apply the action approval policy before external launches, writes, publishes or mutations.

## WLP mode

Use WLP knowledge and domain authorities when relevant. Pricing remains under WLP Financial Sentinel and brand under WLP Brand Expert.
