# 741 Content Quality for Claude

Apply `741/core/skills/content-quality/SKILL.md` and the P1 orchestration contract.

## Claude execution

- Inspect the artifact, audience and objective.
- Flag unsupported claims before polishing, including unsupported quantifiers/generalizations such as `many`, `most`, `leading`, `often`, `typically`, or equivalent language.
- Rephrase unsupported prevalence claims into evidence-neutral wording when possible.
- Use an explicit rubric and explain material deductions.
- Treat scores as evaluation judgments, not predicted commercial outcomes.
- Use WLP Commercial Partner and WLP Brand Expert as WLP voice/brand authorities.
- Do not send, publish or overwrite source content without authorization.
