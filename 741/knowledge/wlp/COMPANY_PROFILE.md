# WLP Company Profile

## Company

Warehouse Logistics Partners (WLP), S.A. is a Panama-based freight forwarding and third-party logistics company.

## Core positioning

WLP operates as a nimble regional logistics partner for warehousing, distribution and fulfillment, using Panama and the Colón Free Zone as a regional distribution hub for Central America, the Caribbean and South America.

Positioning pillars:
- clarity
- speed
- security
- agile/responsive service
- predictable and efficient regional supply chains

## Facilities and presence

- Offices in Panama City and the Colón Free Zone.
- Current Colón Free Zone warehouse operation: approximately 2,800 m².
- Strategic relocation target: approximately 3,500–4,000 m². This is a future target, not current capacity.
- Pallet-rack storage for palletized cargo.
- Bonded-zone operation allows goods to be stored and redistributed regionally, with destination-country duties handled according to the final import destination and applicable rules.

## Services

### 3PL / bonded warehousing
- receiving and inspection
- inventory management
- storage
- order fulfillment
- pick and pack
- cross-docking
- quality control
- shipping and dispatch
- packaging
- labeling
- kitting
- barcode scanning
- inventory reporting

WLP uses barcode scanning and does not position RFID as a current capability.

### Freight forwarding
- ocean FCL
- ocean LCL
- air freight
- land transportation through applicable partners/routes
- export and shipping coordination

## Customer profile

WLP can serve multiple customer types. Historically relevant segments include:
- freight forwarding agencies and overseas logistics partners
- mid-sized companies requiring Panama/Colón Free Zone logistics support
- brands requiring regional warehousing, fulfillment and distribution
- selected larger corporate accounts

Freight forwarding agents from international logistics networks are an important commercial segment.

## Commercial differentiation

- boutique/nimble logistics partner
- regional access from Panama
- bonded warehousing in the Colón Free Zone
- combination of freight forwarding and 3PL execution
- responsive service and operational flexibility
- regional redistribution capability
